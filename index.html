Features

Built so reminders actually land.

A phone call is the one alert people almost never ignore. We made a reminder that feels like one, and then fixed everything that makes other reminder apps fail.

📞

Rings like a real call

Full-screen incoming-call style over your lock screen, with a custom caller name, photo and your own ringtone.

🗣️

Speaks your message

Type it and the app reads it out, or use your own voice note. It repeats until you hang up.

🔁

Repeats until done

Missed it? It calls again, up to your limit. Snooze, reschedule or hang up to mark it Done.

🛡️

Reliability Health

A simple green and red checklist for permissions, battery and sound, each with a Fix button.

🌙

Quiet Hours & Pause All

Go silent at night, pause for an hour, and mark the important ones Urgent so they still get through.

✏️

Edit anything, anytime

Change time, voice or repeat without recreating. Deleted by accident? Restore from the bin within 30 days.

How it works

Set it in seconds. Trust it for good.

Create a call

Add a title, pick the time and choose how loud it should be: full call screen, ring only, or a silent notification.

Test it once

Send a test reminder in 1 minute. Setup is complete only when you have actually heard it ring.

Answer when it rings

Swipe to answer and hear your message. Hang up to mark it Done. Ignore it and it tries again.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it make real phone calls?

No. It shows a call-style reminder on your own phone and labels it clearly. No real calls, SMS or carrier fees.

Does it need internet or an account?

No. Your reminders stay on your phone, with no sign-up and no cloud.

Will it ring when my phone is silent?

By default it respects silent mode and Do Not Disturb. You can allow ringing anyway, globally or per reminder.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8 and above. A setup guide helps on brands with aggressive battery saving.

Is it free?

Yes, you can download Unmissed AI free from Google Play.
